# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Explain disagreements with evidence, give constructive feedback, and make room for different experience levels and backgrounds. Critique the work rather than a person's identity or worth.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized conduct, impersonation, publishing private information, and retaliation for a good-faith report are not acceptable. Respect confidentiality and never include customer data or credentials in community discussion.

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by the project. A designated maintainer may remove harmful content, request a correction, restrict participation, or remove access in proportion to the behavior. Anyone with a conflict of interest should recuse themselves from a report concerning them.

This package has not appointed a conduct contact. Before opening public contributions, the owner must provide a private reporting route and a responsible reviewer. Until then, report concerns privately through the existing contact who provided the package; do not expose a reporter's identity or confidential evidence in a public issue.

This is original project policy text, not an adoption of a third-party code-of-conduct template.
